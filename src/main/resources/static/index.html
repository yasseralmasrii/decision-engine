Agents propose.
The engine rules.

Every action an AI agent wants to take is checked against real records and a fixed set of rules before anything happens. Open a case to follow one ruling from the request to the sealed record.

  • ExecuteConfident, low risk, cheap to be wrong.
  • AskSomething only the requester knows is missing.
  • DeferWaiting will settle it, like CI finishing.
  • EscalateAllowed, but a person with authority decides.
  • RefuseA hard policy forbids it, however confident.
Decision Engine

A decision layer that sits between AI agents and the systems they act on. Agents propose; deterministic rules, checked against real records, decide.
